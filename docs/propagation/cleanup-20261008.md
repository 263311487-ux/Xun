## Limits that remain

This cleanup removes concrete entrance/citation/version errors. It cannot establish why a small audience did not click, whether academic search indexed the pages, or whether the theory outperforms competitors. Model snapshots, item banks/answer keys, real runner/scorer, external registration, real experiments and independent replication remain separate research work. A positive behavioral result would still not by itself establish phenomenal consciousness. External forum/email distribution has no verified publish/send record in this package.
